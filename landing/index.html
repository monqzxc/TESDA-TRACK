How Find my path works

Four stops between a goal and a certificate.

Follow the route at your own pace. Each stop uses what you told us at the one before, so the plan at the end is yours.

1Your goal

Start with what you want to do.

Write it the way you would say it. Gusto kong maging kusinero works as well as I want to become a chef, and so does a goal that names no trade at all, like I want to take care of elderly people abroad.

2Your match

See which qualifications fit, and why.

Matches weigh the meaning of your goal and the words you used, so a goal finds its trade even when it shares no words with the catalog. When a goal has no TESDA equivalent, such as astronaut or lawyer, TESDA Track says so instead of guessing.
