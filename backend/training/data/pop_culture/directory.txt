TVET-FOCUSED POP CULTURE CHARACTER DIRECTORY
Curated edition — 129 entries
Coverage: Marvel, cartoons, anime, Hollywood films, and selected skill-focused television personalities

PURPOSE
This directory uses recognizable screen characters and public media personalities to illustrate practical, employable skills and possible Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) pathways.

FIELDS
Name: Character or public media personality.
Source: Film, series, franchise, or program.
Category: Broad source grouping.
Skills: Practical abilities demonstrated in the source.
TVET Sector: Related occupational or training domain.
Suggested TESDA Qualification / Training Area: Possible Philippine TVET pathway. These are suggested areas, not a guarantee that the wording is an exact current TESDA qualification title.
Why It Matches: Short explanation connecting the character's demonstrated work to the skills and training area.

IMPORTANT NOTES
- Character skills are based on recognizable occupations, repeated activities, or demonstrated competencies in their stories.
- Superpowers are not treated as employable qualifications. Only practical, transferable skills are mapped.
- A few characters have indirect or supporting matches; these are identified through the explanation.
- Some entries are fictional analogies. For formal use, verify qualification titles, codes, entry requirements, and competency standards against current TESDA Training Regulations and official TESDA sources.
- Regulated professions such as medicine, law, and engineering may require university degrees, professional licenses, or other credentials beyond TVET.
- Public figures included as screen personalities are not fictional characters; they are included only where their televised work provides a clear practical-skills example.
